Vantage Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026 · ORB Global LLC

Vantage is a business tool made by ORB Global LLC ("Orb") for store owners and their marketing partners. This page explains what information Vantage handles and why. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What Vantage collects

Account information. Your name, email address, and, if you opt in to text messages, your mobile number. We use these to sign you in and to send you the messages you asked for.

Your store's marketing data. Advertising performance from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, Google Business Profile activity, and public reviews for your store. This data is pulled through reporting connections you or Orb authorized. Vantage reads this data; it does not change your ads or accounts.

What you tell us. Daily check-in answers (how the day went, how many sales), notes about customer leads, and any ad approvals or feedback you give inside the app.

Device information. If you allow notifications, a device token so we can deliver them. We do not collect your location from your phone; the weather you see is based on your store's street address.

How it is used

To show you how your marketing is performing, to compare your store with other stores in your network in aggregate, to recommend what to do next, and to send you the morning brief and evening check-in you signed up for. Orb staff use the same information to support your account.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads inside Vantage. We do not share your store's individual numbers with other store owners; comparisons are shown as network medians and percentiles. Customer names or messages that appear in your lead log are visible only to you and Orb staff supporting your account.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database) and Amazon Web Services (application servers) in the United States, and processed by Anthropic (AI analysis) and Twilio (text messaging) solely to provide the service. Each is bound by its own agreement not to use your data for anything else.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to end messages. Turn off notifications in your phone's settings. Ask us to export or delete your account and its data at any time by emailing privacy@orbglobal.com; we complete deletion within 30 days. Marketing data that belongs to your franchise or to Orb's agency records may be retained as required by those agreements.

Children

Vantage is for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
